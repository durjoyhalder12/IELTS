PAGE 11: Scientific Integrity & Global Priorities (#196 – #200)
============================================================

In our final briefing, we reflect on how societies respond to unprecedented global challenges. When communities face perpetual environmental and technological change, public trust becomes paramount. Journalists, scientists, and policymakers must uphold rigorous contemporary standards and always substantiate every public claim with transparent, verifiable evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------
