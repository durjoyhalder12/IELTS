PAGE 1: Modernising Urban Policy & Education (#1 – #18)
============================================================

Welcome to today's policy briefing. Many rapidly growing cities are trying to ameliorate poor living conditions in crowded districts, because outdated housing plans only exacerbate air pollution and social stress. To mitigate these severe urban pressures, municipal planners must not allow short-term budget cuts to undermine long-term public health. Instead, local governments should bolster public transit and remove administrative red tape that continues to hinder green construction. Fortunately, modern digital tools now facilitate close cooperation between engineers and residents, helping communities foster sustainable neighbourhood design. At the same time, strict zoning rules are needed to curb uncontrolled industrial sprawl. Environmental groups strongly advocate cleaner building codes, and independent auditors carefully scrutinise every municipal contract. A truly comprehensive urban strategy recognises that demographic growth is inevitable, making reliable public infrastructure a pivotal factor in city planning. Clean water and fast transit have become indispensable for modern citizens, whereas the extra cost of green materials is practically negligible over twenty years. Smart sensors are now ubiquitous across metropolitan networks, rapidly rendering older, polluting diesel buses completely obsolete.

  1. ameliorate (v.) — to make a bad situation better (Syn: improve; alleviate | Ant: worsen; aggravate)
  2. exacerbate (v.) — to make a problem worse (Syn: aggravate; intensify | Ant: alleviate; soothe)

  4. undermine (v.) — to weaken gradually (Syn: weaken; erode | Ant: strengthen; support)
