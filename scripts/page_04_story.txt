PAGE 4: Overcoming Obstacles to Sustainable Growth (#58 – #75)
============================================================

Long-term national prosperity depends heavily on modern infrastructure such as reliable power grids, ports, and high-speed rail. Effective environmental legislation supports this progress, especially when paired with a local recycling initiative in schools and offices. However, social progress stalls if society clings to an outdated stereotype or allows workplace prejudice to block talented candidates. Recently, economists reached a broad consensus around a compelling hypothesis: clean-energy investment can shift a nation's entire economic trajectory upward. Indeed, falling battery prices acted as a powerful catalyst for electric transport, marking a historic milestone in industrial history. Even so, high upfront financing remains a major obstacle, and supply-chain delays occasionally cause a temporary setback. Fortunately, a recent scientific breakthrough in solid-state storage resolved the main drawback of heavy battery packs. Skilled engineers are now a country's greatest asset, whereas aging coal plants have become a costly liability during periods of acute water scarcity.
