PAGE 8: Evaluating Reform & Technological Disruption (#137 – #159)
============================================================

When ministries evaluate national education programmes, they must clearly distinguish temporary exam trends from genuine learning gains. To compensate schools in disadvantaged districts, funding formulas should recognise that dedicated teachers constitute the backbone of every classroom. Modern degree courses typically comprise both analytical sciences and creative arts, and updated syllabi increasingly incorporate digital literacy from an early age. While no policy can immediately eliminate every social barrier, targeted public health campaigns have helped eradicate several preventable diseases, much like historic laws helped abolish child labour. Breakthroughs in artificial intelligence continue to trigger rapid industrial shifts and accelerate workplace automation. In fact, cloud computing has helped revolutionise global collaboration and transform how research teams share data. Today, university cohorts are predominantly digital natives, notably in engineering and data science. Costs have risen; consequently, budgets are tighter—nevertheless, innovation continues, albeit unevenly across regions. Urban tech hubs expand rapidly, whereas remote towns adapt more slowly, inevitably facing connectivity gaps. Increasingly, employers demand adaptability, as automation can drastically reshape job roles while leaving overall headcount changed only marginally.
