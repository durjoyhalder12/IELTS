PAGE 9: Public Health, Culture & Digital Ethics (#160 – #176)
============================================================

Modern healthcare raises profound ethical questions about patient autonomy and the growing dependency of hospitals on automated diagnostics. Timely medical intervention can save lives, yet lingering social stigma still prevents many individuals from seeking support for mental well-being. Over the past century, cleaner water and vaccines have dramatically extended the average human lifespan and helped contain many a deadly epidemic by building widespread community immunity. At the same time, balanced nutrition remains vital, because childhood malnutrition and inadequate urban sanitation continue to undermine public health in developing regions. Meanwhile, rapid globalisation and international migration are reshaping modern cities. Preserving local cultural heritage while supporting smooth social assimilation requires open civic dialogue rather than strict media censorship.
